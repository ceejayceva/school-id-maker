Scroll to Bulk Generate. Upload a DepEd SF1 (.xlsx/.xls) or CSV file to import many learners at once.

Download

Use Download all as PDF (A4, 10 cards/page) or ZIP (PNGs) for the whole batch. Single cards use the buttons above the card.

Upload a DepEd School Form 1 (SF1) (.xls/.xlsx) or a CSV of learners to create many IDs at once. SF1 files are auto-detected — school name, year, grade & section, LRN, name, gender, birthdate, address, parent, and contact are read directly from the form. Fields not present in SF1 (school address, school head, position, missing contact) are left blank for you to fill in on the card.
Alternatively upload a plain CSV with columns: name, lrn, birthdate, gender, class, parent, residence, mobile. Optional columns: schoolhead, position, schoolname, schooladd, sy.
